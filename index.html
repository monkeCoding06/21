Rules

Casinos Austria Classic practice rules

Six decks. Every box receives two cards while the bank receives only one initial card. Boxes act one after another; only after all boxes finish does the bank draw its second card and then draw through 16 and stand on 17+. Equal-value cards may be split and re-split; split aces receive one additional card only. Doubling is allowed after the first two cards and after splits except split aces, and receives exactly one card. Three sevens are paid immediately. This practice table uses a 3:2 Black Jack payout; Casinos Austria notes that the actual offered variant may instead use 6:5 or 5:1 and posts the applicable payout at the physical table.

Bet Behind: Drag chips onto another box's Bet Behind field. You follow that box's result and do not control its decisions. If it splits, this simulator divides your behind stake across the resulting hands.

C3: Highest Hearts 300:1 · Jacks in Black 200:1 · Triplets 50:1 · Red Carpet 4:1. 123Jacks: 3 J♠ 300:1 · 3 Jacks 50:1 · 2 J♠ 20:1 · 2 Jacks 10:1 · 1 J♠ 4:1 · 1 Jack 2:1. Bust: dealer bust 2:1. Insurance is offered when the dealer's initial card is an ace and pays 2:1.
